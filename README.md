5. Dashboard Results and Key Insights
The Power BI dashboard presents the major findings of the analysis in an interactive format.
Key outcomes include:
•	Identification of important business trends and patterns.
•	Comparison of performance across relevant categories or segments.
•	Analysis of key performance indicators (KPIs).
•	Data-driven recommendations based on the findings.

Key Insights:
1.	Clothing generates nearly double the unit volume of Footwear and Outerwear combined, anchoring store traffic and digital checkout momentum.
2.	Young adults lead spending, but the customer base is broadly valuable
3.	While 3,116 customers demonstrate habitual loyalty, only 27% have converted to formal subscription memberships, leaving 73% on ad-hoc reordering paths.

Business Recommendations:
1.	Boost subscriptions with clear, exclusive benefits
2.	Reward repeat buyers and move more customers into the Loyal segment
3.	Review discount policy to balance conversion with margin Feature top-rated and best-selling products in campaigns.
4.	Target high-revenue age groups and express-shipping users with tailored offers.
